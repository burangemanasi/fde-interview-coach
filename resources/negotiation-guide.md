# Guide: The Customer Negotiation Round

The customer wants more scope, a faster date, or "can you just add X". Can you
push back and keep the relationship?

**What great looks like**
1. **Clarify first** — what problem X solves, who's asking, and what the real
   deadline is tied to.
2. **Decompose the ask** — split it into must-have now vs. later, and offer a
   smaller first deliverable that still hits the date.
3. **Name the risk** — say what slips or breaks if you say yes to everything.
4. **Communicate the trade-off** — "We can do X if Y moves to phase 2." Give
   options, not a flat no.

**Anti-patterns:** saying yes to keep the peace; hiding behind "it's not in
scope"; negotiating before you know what they actually need.
